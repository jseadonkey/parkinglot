## Goals

- Operational visibility: recurring Slack "agent standup" digest (~every 4 hours UTC) summarizing ingest, scoring/pipeline activity, approvals, and recent audit activity.
- Between-digest momentum: maintenance tick (~15 minutes by default) enqueues pipeline work for parcels that still need scoring/enrichment.
- Deal-quality signals: parking suitability + benchmark hourly rates (pilot YAML comps + optional Postgres parking_rate_comps).
- Owner/outreach: persist owner_outreach_brief and related flows from the pipeline.
- Reliability: SLACK_* on api + worker + beat; internal routes (status, last-digest, digest-preview, digest-now, test-message); CI/workflows; scripts to verify API→Slack vs worker→Slack.

## What we mean by "agents"

- Not one Slack chatbot. The system is services + Celery: Beat schedules slack_agent_digest and enqueue_unscored_pipelines_tick; the worker runs those plus run_parcel_pipeline, ingest, scoring, enrichment, workflows.
- Digest content is built from Postgres (operator standup), not a generic LLM chat loop.
- Owner outreach helper (parking_enrichment) builds OwnerOutreachBrief.
- Optional Slack Socket Mode /parking is separate from digests.
